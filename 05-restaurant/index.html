Our Story

Founded by Chef Marco Rossi in 1985, La Bella Cucina has been bringing the authentic flavors of Rome to our community for four decades. Every recipe is passed down through generations, every ingredient sourced with the utmost care.


Our dining room is a warm embrace — a place where families gather, love stories unfold, and memories are made over bowls of handmade pasta and glasses of fine Chianti.
